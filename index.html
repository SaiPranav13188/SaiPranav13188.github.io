01Native macOS · Swift

Bytewise

A disk space analyzer that explains what's on your Mac in plain words, then cleans up safely.

Nagrik

Photograph a pothole, and AI agents file the complaint with the right government office for you.

06Computer vision · Android

Handsoff

Grab a photo with a fist and throw it to another phone by opening your hand.

MediaPipe hand tracking with smoothing, hysteresis and a 5-frame streak, so flicker never fires a gesture. The native Android app transfers phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct with no internet. The web version pairs with a QR code over a WebSocket relay that never stores files.

How I work

01

Guardrails in code, not prompts

Models choose what to do next. The rules about what they're allowed to do are enforced in the tools, so a model can't talk its way past them.

02

Measure, then claim

Eval harnesses, pinned reference values and test suites come first, and I write down the known limits next to the results.

03

Ship the whole thing

From the agent graph to the database, the mobile app, the deployment and the README, every project is something people can actually use.
